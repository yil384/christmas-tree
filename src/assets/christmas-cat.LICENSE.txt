Adaptation for 集美小喵的魔法圣诞:
- Converted the artist's FBX skin, 24-bone rig and Idle / Walking clips to glTF.
- Merged materials into one vertex-colored skinned mesh, with no external textures.
- Added one subdivision pass and smooth normals to soften the low-poly outline.
- Shortened the body, softened the cheeks/paws and used a silver/cream coat.
- Added small amber eyes attached to the existing head bone, plus a blink morph.

2026-11-13 refinement:
- Kept the artist's exact 24-bone skin bind data and Idle / Walking samples.
- Shortened overall length by 10% and height by 8%, with width retained.
- Rounded and widened the head/cheeks, softened the muzzle, filled the chest,
  and broadened the lower legs/paws without changing relative limb lengths.
- Rebuilt the small eyes with sage irises, soft dark pupils and restrained
  catchlights; the existing Blink morph contract is retained.
- Reshaped the original connected tail into a fuller, tapered curve. Its root
  retains the original topology so no added junction can separate while walking.
- Added a small red kerchief, and quieter matte silver/white vertex colors.
- Bundled as a 329,568-byte GLB: one skinned mesh, one material, no textures
  or runtime third-party asset requests. This is a stylized smooth coat,
  not photoreal fur.
- Reproducible shape refinement: scripts/refine-christmas-cat.py. Its input
  SHA-256 guard selects the pre-refinement asset from repository history.
- Visual QA used offline Blender renders (front, side, three-quarter, walking).
  Those renders do not certify mobile-browser appearance or performance.
