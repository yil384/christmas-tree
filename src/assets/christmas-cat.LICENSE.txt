Adaptation for 集美小喵的魔法圣诞:
- Converted the artist's FBX skin, 24-bone rig and Idle / Walking clips to glTF.
- Merged materials into one vertex-colored skinned mesh, with no external textures.
- Added one subdivision pass and smooth normals to soften the low-poly outline.
- Shortened the body, softened the cheeks/paws and used a silver/cream coat.
- Added small amber eyes attached to the existing head bone, plus a blink morph.
- Bundled as a 345,976-byte GLB; no runtime third-party asset requests.

The original model and its authored animation remain CC0. This is a Quaternius
asset adaptation; it is not an official Disney asset or a Disney character.
