Privacy implementation
The SDK is not modified. The application's classic worker limits fetch,
XMLHttpRequest and importScripts to its own same-origin asset directory.
Only GET asset reads are permitted, without query strings. Fetch omits credentials.
The SDK's built-in metrics POST requests are blocked before network dispatch.
Camera frames and recognition results are neither uploaded nor logged by this app.
